Key Terms — Plain English
AI (Artificial Intelligence)
Computer systems trained on text to understand and generate human language.
LLM (Large Language Model)
The AI engine behind Claude, ChatGPT, and Copilot.
Prompt
What you type to the AI. Better prompts = better results.
Hallucination
When AI states something incorrect with total confidence. Always verify facts.
Context Window
How much the AI can hold in memory per conversation. New chat = blank slate.
Role Prompt
"You are a communications professional at a nonprofit arts center." Sets the tone for everything.
Iteration
Improving output through follow-up. "Make it shorter." "Add more warmth." Keep going.
Copilot
Microsoft's AI built into Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
Generative AI
AI that creates new content — text, images, code — rather than just finding information.
Anonymize
Replace "[Donor Name]" and "[Amount]" for all real data before pasting into any AI tool.
Token
How AI measures text length. ~¾ of a word. Only matters with very long documents.
Asana Intelligence
Asana's built-in AI that generates status reports and flags at-risk tasks.

Trust it as a starting point — not as a source of fact. AI is excellent at drafting language, organizing ideas, and processing text you give it. The most common issue is not enough context. AI only knows what you tell it. 01✓
Set the Stage: Give Claude Your Voice
Teach Claude who The Bushnell is and how you communicate before asking it to write anything.
Claude
›

Before any writing task, open a new Claude conversation and establish context. This "role prompt" sets the tone for everything that follows in that session.

Role Prompt to Start Every Writing Session

"You are a communications professional at The Bushnell Center for the Arts in Hartford, CT — a nonprofit performing arts center with a mission to elevate the human spirit through the arts. Our voice is warm, professional, and community-centered. We serve a diverse audience and are committed to accessibility and inclusion. I'll be asking you to help me draft communications. Always match our tone and avoid corporate jargon."

Once you've set this up, save it as a note or a pinned conversation so you can paste it quickly next time.

02✓
Draft a Donor Communication
Use Claude to write a donor thank-you, appeal, or stewardship note. Refine until it sounds like you.
Claude
›
Example Prompt (after setting role)

"Write a thank-you letter for donors who gave to our education programs last quarter. 3 paragraphs, warm but not saccharine. Acknowledge their impact specifically on youth programming. Include a soft ask to consider renewing at the same level."

Important: Never include real donor names, amounts, or contact information in your prompt. Use placeholders like [Donor Name] and fill them in after.

03✓
Repurpose One Piece of Content into Five
Take a press release or event description and let ChatGPT spin it into social posts, email subject lines, and more.
ChatGPT
›

Paste an existing press release or program description into ChatGPT, then ask:

Content Repurposing Prompt

"Based on the press release below, give me: 1) Three Instagram captions (each under 150 characters, different angles), 2) Two email subject line options, 3) One Facebook post with a question to drive engagement, 4) A 280-character tweet. Keep The Bushnell's warm, arts-centered voice throughout."

One piece of content → 7 deliverables in under 2 minutes. That's the time savings that starts to add up.

Open Microsoft Word and use Draft with Copilot. Grant writing tip: break the grant into sections and work on one at a time.

Grant Needs Statement Prompt

"Write a 3-paragraph needs statement for a community arts education grant. The organization is a nonprofit performing arts center serving diverse Hartford communities. Focus on: arts access gaps for underserved youth, the organization's 15-year track record in education programs, and the specific unmet need for after-school arts programming."

Always review AI-drafted grant content with your Development Director before submitting. AI gives you a starting point — your expertise and knowledge of the funder makes it competitive.

Over time, this becomes your personal shortcut library — and a resource you can share with your team.

After your next Teams meeting, open the meeting in Teams and look for the Recap tab. Copilot automatically generates a summary, action items, and key decisions. You can also ask it questions: "What did we decide about the budget?" or "Who is responsible for the venue contract?"

If transcript/recap isn't appearing, make sure Teams meeting recording and transcription is enabled by your IT admin.

02✓
Turn Data into a Narrative with Claude
Paste anonymized figures into Claude and ask it to write a board-ready narrative explanation.
Claude
›
Example: Variance Narrative Prompt

"I'm writing a board report and need help drafting the narrative for our Q3 expense variance. Here are the anonymized figures: [paste figures with show names replaced by Show A, Show B, etc.]. Write a 3-paragraph narrative explaining the significant variances, likely causes, and any corrective actions planned. Audience is our Board Finance Committee — factual, concise, professional."

Critical: Always have your Finance Director or CFO review AI-generated financial narratives before presenting to the board. AI drafts it — your expertise validates it.

Job Description Prompt

"Write a job description for a [Role Title] at a nonprofit performing arts center. The role reports to [Department Head]. Key responsibilities include [list 3–4]. Open PowerPoint and click Copilot in the Home ribbon. Choose "Create presentation about..." and give it a prompt:
