Let's Be Honest

Questions We Know You're Asking

If you're uncertain, skeptical, or even a little worried about AI — that's completely reasonable. Honest answers to what we hear most.

Will AI replace my job?

At The Bushnell, AI is a tool, not a replacement. The creative judgment, relationship-building, and artistic sensibility that define this work cannot be automated. AI handles repetitive parts of your job so your expertise goes further.

✦ AI amplifies your expertise. It doesn't substitute for it.
Can AI just make things up?

Yes — this is called "hallucination." Never publish, send, or submit AI-generated content without reviewing it yourself. Facts, statistics, donor histories, and dates are especially vulnerable.

✦ Always verify facts. AI drafts; you decide.
Is my data safe when I use these tools?

Claude, ChatGPT, and Copilot are all approved and suitable for professional use — but only when you sign in through your Bushnell-managed workspace with Bushnell SSO, never a personal or public account. Even then, never enter donor names, financial data, personnel records, or confidential contracts. When in doubt, anonymize first.

✦ Sign in with Bushnell SSO, anonymize sensitive data, follow the policy.
Do I need to be technical to use this?

Not at all. Every tool works through plain English conversation. There is no code, no setup, no technical expertise required. If you can write an email, you can use AI.

✦ If you can write an email, you can use AI.
What if I use it wrong or get a bad result?

There's no permanent wrong answer — every session starts fresh. If you get a bad result, rephrase, add context, or ask for a revision. No data is lost and no one is monitoring your prompts.

✦ You can't break it. Bad results are just prompts to refine.
Isn't using AI cheating, or less authentic?

Authenticity comes from your values, relationships, and judgment — not whether a first draft was typed manually. Writers have always used tools: dictation, spell-check, research assistants. What you do with the output is still entirely yours.

✦ Your judgment, values, and voice are what make the work authentic.

Before You Paste Anything

Rules of the Road

Do this

  • —Start with a role prompt — who you are, where you work, what tone you need.
  • —Anonymize before pasting — replace names, amounts, show titles with placeholders.
  • —Review before you send — AI is a first draft, not a final.
  • —Iterate — "shorter," "more warmth," "try again."
  • —Share good prompts — what works for you works for your team.
  • —Only use Claude, ChatGPT, and Microsoft Copilot — any other AI tool needs AI Task Force approval first.

Don't do this

  • —No real donor names or gift amounts — ever.
  • —No HR, salary, or personnel details — generalize only.
  • —Verify facts, stats, and dates — AI hallucinates confidently.
  • —No grant content without expert review — Development Director signs off.
  • —No board, legal, or strategic documents — keep these offline.
  • —Don't use a personal or public ChatGPT or Claude account for work — always sign in through your Bushnell-managed workspace with Bushnell SSO.

House Rules

The Bushnell's AI Policy

◆ Official Policy Statement

The Bushnell recognizes the growing use of Artificial Intelligence, including Generative AI tools, in the workplace. AI can enhance efficiency and productivity, but its use must align with The Bushnell's security, confidentiality, and compliance standards. The Bushnell has established an AI Task Force responsible for overseeing and administering this policy.

🔑 GenAI Technology Approval Process

If you want to use a new AI tool not already approved (e.g. beyond Claude, ChatGPT, and Copilot), you must get approval before using, purchasing, subscribing to, or installing it. All software purchases route through IT.

01
Submit a Proposal

Submit a detailed proposal to the AI Task Force outlining intended use, benefits, and potential risks.

02
Task Force Assessment

The AI Task Force evaluates ethical and legal implications; additional members may be assigned as needed.

03
Approval & Deployment

Only upon approval can the tool be installed, deployed, or subscribed to. IT manages all purchases.

🧠
Human Judgment Always Applies

AI should not be used as a substitute for human judgment. Employees are responsible for verifying the accuracy and reliability of AI-generated outputs before using them in decisions or communications.

🔐
Protection of Confidential Information

Maintain the highest level of privacy with all information exchanged with an AI tool. Do not upload or share data that is confidential, proprietary, or protected by regulation.

👤
Data Privacy & PII

AI tools must not publicly disclose, store, or share PII of employees, customers, patrons, donors, or vendors. All data privacy laws must be followed.

⚖️
Legal Compliance

AI does not absolve you of compliance obligations. All employment laws still apply, including hiring and promotion decisions. Apply your own legal and ethical judgment.

🛡️
Security Best Practices

Apply the same security best practices used for all company and customer data — strong passwords, up-to-date software, and The Bushnell's data retention and disposal policies.

🚫
Compliance with All Company Policies

Use generative AI in accordance with all Bushnell policies, including anti-harassment and anti-discrimination. AI must never be used to create harmful or discriminatory content.

🔍
Fact-Check Everything

Set aside time to fact-check AI-generated materials. Statistics, citations, names, and dates must be verified against primary sources before appearing in any official document.

❓
Questions & the AI Task Force

The AI Task Force oversees this policy, reviews new tool proposals, and provides guidance. If unsure whether a use is appropriate — ask before you act.

The Glossary

Words Worth Knowing

Prompt
The instruction you type. A clearer, more specific prompt almost always produces a better result.
Hallucination
When an AI confidently generates incorrect or invented information. Always verify before using it in official documents.
Context Window
The maximum text an AI can "hold in mind" during one conversation. Larger windows can process entire long reports.
Token
The unit AI models use to measure text — roughly ¾ of a word. Longer documents use more of the model's "memory."
System Prompt
An instruction at the start of a conversation that shapes how the AI behaves throughout — like "You are a nonprofit development director..."
Knowledge Cutoff
The date after which a model has no training data, unless it has real-time web search.
Prompt Chaining
Breaking a complex task into a sequence of smaller prompts, each building on the last, for better results.
Multimodal
An AI that can process images, audio, video, and documents — not just text.
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
A technique where AI searches specific documents before answering, so responses are grounded in your actual content.
LLM (Large Language Model)
The underlying technology behind most AI tools — trained on vast text to predict and generate language.
Fine-Tuning
Training a model further on a specific dataset to make it more expert in a particular domain.
Guardrails
Built-in safety rules that prevent an AI from producing harmful, false, or inappropriate content.
Temperature
A setting controlling how creative vs. predictable AI output is — low for precision, high for brainstorming.
Iteration
Refining AI output through follow-up prompts rather than accepting the first response — the single most impactful habit.
Anonymize
Replacing real names, amounts, or identifiers with placeholders before pasting sensitive content.
Grounding
When an AI ties responses to specific verified sources rather than general training data, reducing hallucination.
